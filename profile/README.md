# OptiTech Solutions

**OptiTech Solutions** is a student-led software development team from the University of Ghana. We build functional and scalable solutions across mobile, web, and system platforms.

## About Us

We are a group of students passionate about applying software engineering skills to solve real-world problems. Our work spans client-based applications, internal tools, and learning-focused builds.

## Projects

### Bel Depot System

Our first major project is a booking and inventory management system developed for a beverage distribution company. It includes:

- Depot scheduling and booking workflows
- Inventory tracking and stock control
- Multi-user access with defined roles (customers and depot managers)  
- Reporting capabilities

This project is **private** due to its enterprise nature and intended use within a commercial environment.
